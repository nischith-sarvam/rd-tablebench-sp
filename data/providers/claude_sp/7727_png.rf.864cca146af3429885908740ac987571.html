INCREASED RISK - People who have a history of polyps on prior colonoscopy
Risk category When to test Recommended test(s) Comment
People with small rectal hyperplastic polyps Same age as those at average risk Colonoscopy, or other screening tests Those with hyperplastic polyps
People with 1 or 2 small (less than 1 cm) tubular adenomas with low-grade dysplasia 5 to 10 years after the polyps are removed Colonoscopy Time between tests should be based on other factors
People with 3 to 10 adenomas, or a large (1 cm or larger) adenoma, or any adenomas with high-grade dysplasia or villous features 3 years after the polyps are removed Colonoscopy Adenomas must have been completely removed
People with more than 10 adenomas on a single exam Within 3 years after the polyps are removed Colonoscopy Doctor should consider possibility of genetic syndrome
People with sessile adenomas that are removed in pieces 2 to 6 months after adenoma removal Colonoscopy If entire adenoma has been removed

Test Pros Cons
Flexible sigmoidoscopy Fairly quick and safe Usually doesn't require full bowel prep Looks at only about a third of the colon Can miss many polyps and some cancers
Colonoscopy Can usually look at the entire colon Can usually remove polyps Can miss small polyps Full bowel prep needed More expensive than some other tests Sedation usually needed
Double-contrast barium enema (DCBE) Can usually see the entire colon Relatively safe Can miss small polyps Full bowel prep needed Cannot remove polyps during testing
CT colonography (virtual colonoscopy) Fairly quick and safe Can usually see the entire colon Does not require sedation Can miss small polyps Full bowel prep needed Cannot remove polyps during testing
Guaiac-based fecal occult blood test (gFOBT) No direct risk to the colon No bowel prep Can miss many polyps and some cancers May have false positive test results Dietary restrictions required before testing

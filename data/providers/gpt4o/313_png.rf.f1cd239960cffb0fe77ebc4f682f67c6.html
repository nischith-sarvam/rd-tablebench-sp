Transfer Goals Understand MLA Format • Employ team collaboration with a focus on incorporating multiple lenses and perspectives Be able to write a claim/ thesis statement based on topic research • Write a summary of research • Determine credibility of sources after completing research Applying alternate/competing arguments to a claim • Find evidence to support a claim • Develop line of reasoning in argument Incorporate visuals, data, and media into argument and presentation • Research counterarguments to strengthen claim/thesis Create a team multimedia presentation • Identify and research a real-world problem or issue • After identifying a realworld problem, develop a solution or resolution to the problem Analyze stimulus materials from the College Board • Write a 2000 word research paper incorporating stimulus material

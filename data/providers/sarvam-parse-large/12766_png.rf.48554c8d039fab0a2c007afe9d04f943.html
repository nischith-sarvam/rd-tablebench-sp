Reading Read the text "Wayne Rooney: Captain of England". - Use the text to help you answer the questions. Remember to write in complete sentences starting with a capital letter. - You may find it easier to write the answers in your book rather than on the sheet. - Check your answers. You don't always have to write the exact words, just the right idea! Spellings Focus on these words with suffixes: information adoration sensation preparation admiration imagination pollution multiplication exploration expectation Complete the three spelling tasks on different days. You can use the guidelines to help you practise the spellings in your best joined handwriting.

Criteria Phase 1 Phase 2 and 2*
Inclusion criteria • ≥18 years of age • RIFR resistant by the GeneXpert Mtb/RIF assay • Enrolled within 1 week of RIF resistance determination by GeneXpert • Not treated for TBR for at least 4 weeks • Intend to remain in Moldova for 24 months • >5 years of age • Suspected or confirmed clinically active TB disease (at least one of the following): • AFB+ sputum smear positive within prior 7 days • GeneXpert positive within prior 7 days • Clinical suspicion of TB • And suspected or confirmed DR-TB (defined as at least one of the following): • Received >1 month of treatment for a prior TB episode • Suspected of failing standard TB treatment • Close contact with a known DR-TB case • Diagnosed with RIF-R within the past 30 days • Previously diagnosed with MDR-TB and suspected of failing a standard MDR-TB treatment regimen
Exclusion criteria • Pregnant women • Institutionalized or incarcerated patients • Patients unable to produce 0.5 mL of sputum for the study testing • Pregnant women • Institutionalized or incarcerated patients • Patients unable to produce 0.5 mL of sputum for the study testing • Started treatment for the current TB episode more than 14 days prior to the enrollment date
